Practical solutions for Hawaiʻi businesses, homes, and properties.

Aloha Technology Services LLC is locally owned and operated on Hawaiʻi Island. We help businesses, homeowners, vacation rentals, and property managers build websites, implement automation and AI, improve operations, modernize technology, provide owner-directed property field support, coordinate qualified local vendors, provide residential home inspections, and complete practical property projects.

Technology, websites, automation, and AI

Services include responsive website development, custom applications, practical business automation, responsible AI implementation, mesh Wi-Fi, television and connected-device installation, training resources, and technical support.

Property and operational support

We provide owner-directed property field services and residential home inspections for buyers, sellers, and homeowners, along with workflow optimization, reporting and analysis, vendor coordination, project assistance, and handyman services for minor work that does not require a licensed trade professional. Property Field Services do not include leasing, tenant placement, rent handling, or property management.
